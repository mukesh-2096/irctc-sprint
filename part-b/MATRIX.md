# IRCTC Impact vs Effort Matrix

| Feature                      | Impact    | Effort    | Quadrant      |
| ---------------------------- | --------- | --------- | ------------- |
| Persistent Search Filters    | High      | Low       | Quick Win     |
| Mobile Booking Redesign      | High      | Medium    | Quick Win     |
| Waitlist Notification System | High      | Medium    | Major Project |
| Tatkal Virtual Queue         | Very High | Very High | Major Project |
| Persistent Seat Selection    | Medium    | Medium    | Fill-In       |
| Refund & TDR Assistant       | Medium    | Low       | Quick Win     |

---

# Justifications

## Persistent Search Filters — Quick Win

This issue affects nearly every IRCTC user during train searches. The solution mainly requires frontend persistence and minor backend validation improvements.

---

## Mobile Booking Redesign — Quick Win

Most IRCTC users access the platform through mobile devices. Improving responsive layouts can significantly reduce booking frustration with moderate engineering effort.

---

## Waitlist Notification System — Major Project

This feature affects millions of waitlisted passengers. However, it requires prediction systems, notification infrastructure, and historical data processing.

---

## Tatkal Virtual Queue — Major Project

Tatkal crashes impact lakhs of users daily and directly affect revenue-critical booking windows. The solution requires major backend infrastructure redesign.

---

## Persistent Seat Selection — Fill-In

The issue impacts a smaller subset of booking sessions. The solution requires moderate frontend and backend synchronization work.

---

## Refund & TDR Assistant — Quick Win

Refund confusion is common among users, but the solution mostly involves guided UI improvements and better information presentation.
